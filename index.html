Scaling Spectral Richness: Residual Self-Attention Mechanisms for Genre-Conditioned Audio Diffusion on Small Datasets
Designed and trained an 89.5M-parameter genre-conditioned spectrogram diffusion model from scratch on GTZAN using a single T4 GPU. Introduced zero-initialized residual self-attention and showed it improves harmonic richness even when MSE loss is unchanged — revealing a gap in standard audio-generation evaluation metrics.
